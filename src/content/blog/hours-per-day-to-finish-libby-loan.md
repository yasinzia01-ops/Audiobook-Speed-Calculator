<div class="answer-box"><p><strong>Quick Answer:</strong> Divide your audiobook's total hours by your remaining loan days, then divide again by your speed multiplier. Example: 12-hour book, 5 days left, at 1.5x speed = 12 / 5 / 1.5 = 1.6 hours per day (96 minutes). Speed is the easiest way to lower this number.</p></div>

## How Do You Calculate Hours Per Day for a Libby Loan?

The formula to find your required daily listening time is simple. You need three pieces of information: the audiobook's total length in hours, the number of days remaining on your loan, and the playback speed you plan to use.

<div class="formula-box"><p>Daily listening hours required</p><code>Book hours / Loan days / Speed = Hours per day</code></div>

For a precise, instant calculation using your actual numbers, use our [Libby Speed Calculator](/libby-speed-calculator/). It handles the math automatically and shows your required daily listening time at 1x, 1.5x, and 2x speeds side by side.

## How Many Hours Per Day at 1.5x Speed?

At 1.5x speed, every hour of listening covers 1.5 hours of audio. This reduces your daily listening requirement by 33% compared to 1x. A 15-hour book that requires 43 minutes per day at 1x drops to just 29 minutes per day at 1.5x on a 21-day loan.

## How Many Hours Per Day at 2x Speed?

At 2x speed, your daily listening requirement is cut in half compared to 1x. A 20-hour audiobook that requires 57 minutes per day at 1x on a 21-day loan needs only 29 minutes per day at 2x. This makes even very long audiobooks achievable on tight loan periods with modest daily listening commitments.

## What Is a Realistic Daily Listening Goal for Most People?

Most audiobook listeners average 30-60 minutes per day during commutes, exercise, chores, or wind-down time before sleep. According to the <a href="https://www.audiopub.org/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Audio Publishers Association</a>, the average US audiobook listener consumes approximately 15 hours per month, which works out to around 30 minutes per day.

At 30 minutes per day at 1.5x speed on a 21-day loan, you can cover 31.5 hours of audio content - more than enough for virtually any audiobook. At 2x speed with the same 30 minutes per day, you can cover 42 hours of audio in a single 21-day loan period.

## What Should You Do If You Miss a Day of Listening?

Missing a day increases your required daily listening time for the remaining days. Recalculate by dividing the remaining unlistened audio by the remaining loan days. Often, bumping speed up by 0.25x (for example, from 1.5x to 1.75x) is enough to compensate for a missed day without dramatically increasing the daily commitment.

If you are falling significantly behind, check whether your loan is eligible for renewal - see our guide on [how to renew a Libby audiobook loan](/how-to-renew-libby-audiobook-loan/) for step-by-step instructions.
