## What is the formula to calculate time saved at 1.5x speed?

<div class="abc-answer"><span class="aa-label">Direct Answer</span><p>Time Saved = Original Length × (1 − 1 ÷ Speed). At 1.5x: Time Saved = Original Length × 0.333. For a 12-hour audiobook: 12 × 0.333 = 4 hours saved. Adjusted listening time = 12 ÷ 1.5 = 8 hours. This formula works for any speed.</p></div>
<div class="formula-box"><div class="formula-text">Time Saved = Original Length × <span>(1 − 1 ÷ Speed)</span></div><div class="formula-note">At 1.5x: multiply original length by 0.333 · At 2x: multiply by 0.5 · At 1.25x: multiply by 0.2</div></div>

Examples using the formula:

-   **6-hour audiobook at 1.5x:** 6 × 0.333 = **2 hours saved** → adjusted time: 6 ÷ 1.5 = 4 hours
-   **9-hour audiobook at 1.5x:** 9 × 0.333 = **3 hours saved** → adjusted time: 6 hours
-   **12-hour audiobook at 1.5x:** 12 × 0.333 = **4 hours saved** → adjusted time: 8 hours
-   **15-hour audiobook at 1.5x:** 15 × 0.333 = **5 hours saved** → adjusted time: 10 hours
